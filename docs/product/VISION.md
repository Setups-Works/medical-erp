# Product vision

**MENO — Pharmacy Management, Simplified.** One platform for retail medical shops, wholesale distributors and pharmacy chains in India.

## Problem

Most pharmacies run on old desktop software: single-PC, no real multi-branch, weak expiry control, manual Schedule H1 registers, painful GST returns, no owner visibility from outside the shop. Cloud tools exist but stop working when the internet drops — unacceptable at a counter with a queue.

## Who we serve

| Persona | Needs most |
|---|---|
| Owner (1–10 branches) | Today's sales/cash/margin from anywhere, stock & expiry control, outstanding |
| Billing operator / pharmacist | Fast keyboard billing, right batch, never blocked by the internet |
| Inventory manager | Quick purchase entry (CSV import), expiry returns, reorders |
| Accountant | Clean GST data, ledgers, Tally export |
| Wholesale distributor | Orders, schemes, credit control, e-invoice, collections |

## Principles

1. Enterprise-grade security and tenant isolation.
2. Billing and stock are always right — immutable posted documents, movement ledger.
3. Counter never stops — offline desktop billing with safe sync.
4. Fast and keyboard-first at the counter; calm and clear in the back office.
5. One codebase, two editions.
6. Compliance helpers built in (H1 register, licences, GST) — but MENO does not replace the pharmacist's legal responsibility.

## Success metrics (pilot)

- Bill in < 20 s for a 5-line prescription by a trained operator.
- 0 stock or GST discrepancies found in pilot audit.
- 0 cross-tenant data incidents.
- 3 pilot pharmacies (2 retail, 1 wholesale) running daily for 1 month.
